0002 · Duplicate reports require evidence of the same cause

owner Alex (gpt-6.1-sol) proposed reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

This proposal would supersede accepted decision 0001 after desk approval. Decision 0001 is preserved unchanged. The review of #4471 shows why matching subjects alone does not identify duplicate problems.

Before merging, look up the tenant locale and booking form, record the report channel, and check the report body against the candidate cause. The intake tag is a guess from the browser locale, so it cannot replace tenant context. Keep shared-parser failures separate from booking-v3 hint failures even when their subjects match. Different subjects may still describe the same cause.

An unknown tenant or a contradictory symptom requires investigation. Preserve the source report and its id instead of silently assigning it to a familiar cause. Timing discrepancies in the export remain visible follow-up evidence; this proposal does not establish a timezone or DST bug.

Count unique report ids. Retain received and merged timestamps separately, preserve their declared offsets, and distinguish a bulk merge from an increase in incoming reports. Recompute counts from a consistent export and rebuild the KB before delivery. Refresh drafts when new reports arrive; accepted historical decisions remain immutable.

The desk must approve the new triage policy and the engineering teams must verify repairs on both paths. This page records a proposed process change and does not claim an implemented production fix.
