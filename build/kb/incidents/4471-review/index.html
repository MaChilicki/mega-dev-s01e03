What to do now

Keep the shared parser and booking-v3 hint as separate engineering issues. The parser must respect the tenant date order; booking-v3 must display the same order its form accepts. Fixing one path does not prove the other is repaired. Validate both using the report evidence and resulting invites. The scenario mentions about 300,000 calendar users; that is product reach, not the number of affected users measured by this export.

The subject-only triage rule and accepted decision 0001 are historical evidence. Their old review dates exceed the KB freshness threshold. Keep the accepted decision immutable. Proposed decision 0002 would supersede it: compare tenant, locale, form and channel, and read contradictory report bodies before merging. A matching subject is neither necessary nor sufficient evidence of a shared cause. The proposal has not yet been accepted by the desk.
